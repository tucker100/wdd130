Rafting Trips

Whether it is your first time on the water or your fiftieth, we have a trip for you. Every trip includes a certified guide, safety gear, and a safety briefing.

Featured Trips

Los Dientes del Diablo

Our most extreme run takes you through the famous "Devil's Teeth," a series of Class IV rapids with towering waves and tight drops. Expect to get soaked, paddle hard, and earn serious bragging rights.

Full day · Class IV · Ages 16+

Emerald Canyon Float

Drift between towering canyon walls on calm turquoise water. This relaxing trip is perfect for families, photographers, and anyone who wants to enjoy the river at a gentle pace, with time to swim.

Half day · Class I–II · Ages 6+

Raft Masters Challenge

Work as a team through a full day of Class III rapids, learning paddle commands and river reading from our lead guides. A great choice for groups and team-building outings.

Full day · Class III · Ages 12+
