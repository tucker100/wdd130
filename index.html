Hello! My name is McMavin Tucker, and I'm from Warri, Delta State, in the heart of Nigeria's Niger Delta region. Delta State is known for its rich cultural diversity, home to the Urhobo, Isoko, Itsekiri, Ijaw, and Anioma peoples, each bringing their own traditions, languages, and festivals to the region. Warri itself is a vibrant, bustling city, well known for its lively spirit, unique Pidgin English expressions, and strong sense of community. The state also plays a major role in Nigeria's oil and gas industry, sitting at the center of the country's petroleum production. I'm currently exploring the world of web programming and software development, and I'm genuinely excited about it. Learning how to build things from scratch and from structuring a simple webpage to eventually creating full applications has been both challenging and rewarding, and I'm looking forward to growing my skills further.

Nigeria

Nigeria is a culturally rich West African country of over 200 million people and 250+ ethnic groups. Its landscape ranges from Idanre's granite hills to the Niger Delta's waterways, including Delta State, home to cities like Warri reflecting Nigeria's nickname, "Giant of Africa."
