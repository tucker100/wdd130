Delta State is known for its rich cultural diversity, home to the Urhobo, Isoko, Itsekiri, Ijaw, and Anioma peoples, each bringing their own traditions, languages, and festivals to the region. Warri itself is a vibrant, bustling city, well known for its lively spirit, unique Pidgin English expressions, and strong sense of community. The state also plays a major role in Nigeria's oil and gas industry, sitting at the center of the country's petroleum production.
