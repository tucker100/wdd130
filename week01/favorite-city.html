Uyo

Uyo is one of my favorite cities. It is the capital of Akwa Ibom State in Nigeria. The city is known for its role in the oil and gas industry, but it also has a lively culture, great food, and a strong sense ofcommunity.

What I love most about Uyo is the energy of the people and the mix of modern city life with the natural beauty of the surrounding waterways. There is always something happening, whether it's a local market, a festival, or just the everyday hustle that gives the city its character.
